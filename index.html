LexNova Privacy Policy

Last Updated: August 12, 2026

LexNova ("we", "our", or "us") respects your privacy and is committed to protecting the information provided while using the LexNova mobile application.

1. Information We Collect

LexNova may ask users to provide basic academic information when entering the application. This information may include:

  • Name
  • Degree
  • Section
  • Roll Number
  • Semester

The information requested by the app may be updated or changed in the future as new features are added.

2. How We Use Information

The information provided by users is used to personalize the application experience and to display relevant academic content, including information related to the user's degree, section, roll number, and semester.

3. Account Registration

LexNova does not currently require users to create a traditional account or provide a password for registration.

4. Information Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or trade users' personal or academic information to third parties.

Information may only be disclosed when required by applicable law or when necessary to protect the security and integrity of the application.

5. Data Security

We take reasonable measures to protect information provided through LexNova. However, no electronic storage or transmission method can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

6. Data Retention and Deletion

LexNova does not currently require users to create an online account. If information is stored by the application, users may contact us to request information about its deletion or handling.

7. Children's Privacy

LexNova is intended for educational purposes. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children in violation of applicable laws.

8. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time as the application develops or new features are introduced. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated date.

9. Contact Us

If you have any questions or concerns about this Privacy Policy or LexNova, please contact the developer through the contact information provided on the Google Play Store listing.
